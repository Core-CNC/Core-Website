Who we are

An independent CNC advisory, built for manufacturers.

Core-CNC is a new advisory business helping manufacturing companies improve how they use CNC machining. Whether that means refining day-to-day CNC practices, choosing the right tooling for the job, or specifying new equipment, we bring hands-on, shop-floor experience — not just theory.

We're starting local, working with businesses across the Midlands, with plans to grow from there.

What we do

Advisory built around three things that move the needle

01

CNC Practices & Process Review

An honest look at how your CNC operation runs today, and where efficiency, consistency and part quality can be improved without a full overhaul.

02

Tooling Selection & Optimisation

Independent guidance on cutting tools and tooling strategy, matched to your materials, machines and production goals — not tied to any one supplier.

03

Equipment Advisory

Impartial advice before you commit to new machine tools or equipment, so the investment actually fits how your shop works.

Why Core-CNC

Local, independent, and grounded in practice

  • Local & accessible. Midlands-based, with on-site visits — not a call centre or a national chain.
  • Independent advice. No ties to a particular tooling or machine supplier, so recommendations are about what's right for you.
  • Practical experience. Advice grounded in real shop-floor knowledge, not just spreadsheets and sales decks.
